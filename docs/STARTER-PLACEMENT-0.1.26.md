# Natural village starter placement (0.1.26)

The natural-colony search now checks a dense grid of loaded land around the located structure and plans the house, farm and town hall together before creating the colony. It prefers the original 11×11 farm but falls back to a 7×7 farm where space is limited. It may clear naturally generated, non-persistent leaves and replaceable vegetation in the plot; it will not clear logs, player-placed leaves, containers, water or other solid structures. Terrain may be levelled by up to two blocks. If no safe pair of plots exists it retries after 30 seconds, without force-loading terrain.

The same plot rules and compact-farm fallback apply to `/colony spawn` and `/colony repair <id>`. Existing colonies that were not created when discovery failed should appear when visited again; if a colony already exists but lacks starters, use repair. The search is conservative: a very dense forest, steep or wet land, or unloaded chunks may still prevent a site from being found. Removing a few trees or visiting with more surrounding chunks loaded may help. Do not use `reset_citizens` unless you intend to clear citizen records.

Manual verification: back up a world, locate several culture village centers including forest and sloped examples, and wait up to 30 seconds near each. Check for the discovery message, town hall, house, farm, three villagers and their workstations; make sure existing houses and water were not replaced. Repeat after reload and with two nearby players to ensure one colony per structure. Try a deliberately blocked/water-only location and verify no partial colony is made. In an existing incomplete colony, use repair and check for a compact farm if no full-sized plot is available.
