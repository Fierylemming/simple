# Starter miners (0.1.28)

New colonies, natural or player-founded, start with four villagers: farmer, woodcutter, builder and miner. They also receive one stone pickaxe in abstract colony stock and attempt to place a furnace. Miner is now a free starter profession, including when old colonies load. Existing populated colonies do not receive a new citizen automatically; reassign an adult in the villager menu if wanted. For an existing colony without a pickaxe, put a stone pickaxe in a nearby colony chest or use `/colony give stone_pickaxe 1` while inside the colony. `/colony repair <id>` can place a missing starter furnace when a safe site exists; it does not add citizens to an already populated colony.

The miner needs an unclaimed loaded furnace and a stone or better pickaxe in colony stock. The current tool is a shared availability check: it is not equipped or damaged by mining. Gold ore requires an iron or better pickaxe. The quarry is remote, cycling through underground locations and mining only explicitly supported natural stone and ore types; output is deposited in colony stock/chests. To reduce damage it skips known buildings, generated structures, shallow blocks, containers, fluids and blocks next to non-natural space or materials. It can report STUCK while searching and may not find a site in a crowded or unloaded colony. This remains heuristic: player-placed stone buried in natural stone is not distinguishable, so back up valuable builds. The miner is not a physical pathfinding miner yet.

New queued house plans now use cobblestone for the bottom wall and oak logs above it. Existing pre-update queued houses keep their original all-log plan, including after a reload; finished houses are unchanged. Only houses currently have working builder plans.

Manual verification: back up the world. Spawn a new colony on open ground and confirm four adults, a furnace and one stone pickaxe in `/colony warehouse`. Use `/colony status` to check the miner. Wait while chunks remain loaded and compare cobblestone counts; break the furnace and confirm work pauses, then replace it. Queue a new house with `/colony build house`, check that its lower wall consumes cobblestone and its upper wall uses logs. Save and reload an older partially built house and verify it continues its original plan. Check nearby structures and chests remain intact. These are not yet in-game verified.
