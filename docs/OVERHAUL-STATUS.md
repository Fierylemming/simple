# Living-village overhaul status

The overhaul remains **in development and unreleased**. For the current implementation, limitations and test history, see [OVERHAUL-WIP.md](OVERHAUL-WIP.md) and [TEST-BUILD.md](TEST-BUILD.md). The old 0.1.19 foundation status was obsolete: non-house construction and physical professions have since been implemented in part, but the full overhaul is not finished.

Outstanding gaps include physical handling of births, fire-spread protection (which would need a mixin), complete protection of unnamed animals, more robust navigation and routines, wider building and growth coverage, full diplomacy and warfare, generalized Create schematic support, and validation in normal single-player and multiplayer worlds. Daily food is now eaten physically from shared storage, mob explosions and pistons cannot break colony property while player-caused destruction and sieges still can, and completed harbors, markets and temples have real effects (trade visits and reputation). Research has a physical builder-delivery workflow, but still needs live-world validation. Wooden costs are species-agnostic families resolved against stored stock, and colonies adopt the wood that grows around them, so expansion no longer depends on finding oak; mixed-biome behaviour and mixed-forest preference still need in-game verification. Colony trades are now data-driven per job, but prices, reputation effects and market behaviour are not; player-placed blocks are remembered per colony, but that is best effort rather than a claim system. Existing GameTests exercise specific supported cases; they are not proof of end-to-end reliability.
