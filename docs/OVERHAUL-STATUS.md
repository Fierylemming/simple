# Living-village overhaul status

The overhaul remains **in development and unreleased**. For the current implementation, limitations and test history, see [OVERHAUL-WIP.md](OVERHAUL-WIP.md) and [TEST-BUILD.md](TEST-BUILD.md). The old 0.1.19 foundation status was obsolete: non-house construction and physical professions have since been implemented in part, but the full overhaul is not finished.

Outstanding gaps include physical handling of all colony consumption and research, complete protection of player property and unnamed animals, more robust navigation and routines, wider building and growth coverage, full diplomacy and warfare, generalized Create schematic support, and validation in normal single-player and multiplayer worlds. Existing GameTests exercise specific supported cases; they are not proof of end-to-end reliability.
