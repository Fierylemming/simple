# Overhaul in progress — not a release

The user requested a single completed release. This branch is a work in progress, not a distribution-ready mod or jar recommendation. Do not treat compilation as proof of in-game correctness. Back up worlds and use a disposable test world if testing the branch.

## Decisions

- `/time` catches up daily food and other daily effects, but does not fast-forward walking, gathering or building. Large skips can starve citizens.
- A new settlement begins with a town hall, food, a farm and four workstation blocks; there are no initial beds. Villagers should shelter in the hall or houses at night.
- Three level-one houses per culture; expansion should respond to needs and affordable materials rather than a fixed day timer.
- User requested a single completed release, including accurate physical work for every profession and social behaviours.

## Work started

Expansion now checks on normal colony ticks, reports blockers and does not stall on the previously unsupported next-building type. It queues an affordable house if shelter capacity is low, including overcrowding at the population cap. Housing searches loaded plots on a dense grid, considers the cost of each house variant, and requires a clear entrance; it does not spend stock merely to plan. Construction can craft a small set of oak components and has preliminary movement, with an occupied-placement check. Legacy unsupported blueprints no longer block house expansion. Only explicitly designated loaded containers are scanned; unmarked player containers are excluded. Existing colony virtual stock is unchanged, but previously auto-scanned chests must be opted in again with a Building Tool (sneak-use each chest/barrel/shulker half, requiring leader status or 20 reputation). Claims persist on the block entity at its original position; replacing or moving the container requires a fresh claim. Cached stock is refreshed against loaded inventories before consumption. Current v10 houses retain the v6 three-footprint plans (including an L shape) and distinct culture-dependent walls and roof silhouettes, using oak and cobblestone; older versions remain available on reload. These designs and their in-game accessibility are not yet verified. New settlements attempt to use the starter structure as a bedless town hall; a preliminary nighttime shelter navigation behaviour and crop harvesting/replanting using actual drops were added. Farmers work within five horizontal blocks of a completed designated farm near their claimed composter and craft bread from harvested wheat; uncooked wheat is no longer counted as a meal. Woodcutters now approach and cut one block at a time for short recognizable trees, recheck safety between cuts, and use stock saplings rather than creating them. Workstation-based crafting jobs now approach the claimed POI; a basic stock-backed baker has been added. The butcher no longer fabricates meat: it attacks excess unnamed adult cows or pigs near a completed butcher hut, leaves a pair, and collects only actual unmodified livestock drops from its own attack when they spawn within reach. It feeds eligible breeding pairs with real stock and relies on vanilla animal AI to produce offspring, subject to a small herd cap. These are not yet verified in a running game.

## Required before release

- Verify/fix the startup town hall, compact farm, pathfinding, door placement, roofs, resource accounting and 15 house plans in-game, including reload and blocked/unloaded sites. The initial town hall and house designs are provisional and not historically complete.
- Test the builder, forester, mine, smithy, butcher, bakery, leatherworking and fishing worksite shells around claimed POIs, including site separation, obstructed/legacy sites, materials, pathfinding and reloads. The v7 smithy includes a builder-crafted adjacent furnace; v6 and older unsupported smithy/butcher placeholders remain unchanged on load. The v8 fishing worksite is a small hut, not a dock; it is queued only around a fisherman's claimed barrel on a clear dry plot near a small two-deep exposed water body. Earlier unsupported harbor placeholders remain unchanged. Version 9 adds compact bakery and leatherworker shells at claimed workstations; v8 and older placeholders retain their original unsupported plans. A completed worksite elsewhere no longer suppresses another worker's separate worksite, and worksite plots need a clear approach to their entrance. The water check does not prove shore access or natural fish spawning, and the colony does not create or place the barrel automatically. Version 10 adds warehouse and barracks shells at claimed courier and defender workstations; the warehouse barrel must first be explicitly shared. Older unsupported warehouse/barracks placeholders are unchanged. Improve site planning and growth for additional building types, including farms and civic buildings; the supported worksite shells are not a complete set of building plans. Colony tiers can advance when population and completed-building thresholds are met; a proposed radius is rejected if it would overlap another colony. This is not full territory protection: terrain and player structures within the expanded radius still need safe handling and in-game verification.
- Finish and test physical tree harvesting. A preliminary short-tree implementation now saves partial-tree progress, revalidates safety after reload, and uses real axes with durability; hand cutting remains a compatibility fallback for old saves. Focused GameTests cover resuming after an entity save, axe wear and refusing to continue after new construction; broader testing is still needed. A preliminary miner now walks a descending quarry and uses a real pickaxe; its focused GameTest has passed but quarry safety, reload, site exhaustion and natural-world navigation still need coverage. Avoid damage to buildings or player property; natural-block recognition remains heuristic.
- Finish and test every profession's real supply-chain behaviour, including butcher, leatherworker, blacksmith, baker, fisherman, courier, soldier/guard and any required shepherd/wool system. The blacksmith now crafts capped spare stone or iron tools from real inputs at a claimed workstation, including sticks from oak stock; it now uses a real adjacent furnace in a designated blacksmith building, consumes actual fuel and ore, and persists batch ownership on the furnace. Automated smelting is limited to raw iron and requires broader integration testing. The leatherworker crafts a single spare chestplate at its claimed workstation using eight leather. Idle guards and soldiers can walk to designated accessible storage and equip an actual leather chestplate and stone or iron sword without replacing existing gear; equipment components survive pickup, and swords wear during combat. Smiths keep one spare sword from actual materials when defenders exist; weapons can be stowed and reused across job changes. This is only a first equipment path, not a full delivery or armory system. A preliminary fisherman gathers only actual excess wild cod or salmon from a reachable shoreline near a completed harbor (player-designated or constructed) and a claimed barrel; named, bucketed and persistent fish are protected. It is hand gathering, not vanilla rod fishing, and unmarked player fish cannot be reliably identified; the remaining wild fish do not breed, so natural spawning is needed. A preliminary courier now carries a persisted stack between storage containers and a designated hub; other stock access is still remote, but unmarked player containers are excluded. All professions can now be selected from the villager menu with the relevant reputation and research gates. Leader commands can make loaded soldiers or guards follow or stop following; commands are session-local and do not survive an entity reload. Combat workers can defend without a workstation. Current nonstarter professions are not distribution-ready.
- Idle adults can now approach nearby idle colony neighbors for vanilla gossip; broader social interactions and robust day/night navigation remain incomplete. Shelter selection now checks a solid floor, clear headroom, nearby roof and reachable interior, with fallback to other completed houses or halls. A bounded walking-height flood check now rejects roofed spaces with obvious wall openings; it treats doors as closable boundaries and is not a full proof of enclosure or protection from hostile door breaking. It does not reserve space or guarantee villagers remain inside all night. The GameTests exercise a bedless hall and two villagers avoiding a roofless marker and sealed house for a constructed house; full-night and in-game tests remain necessary. Address beds and actual food production/cooking beyond wheat counts.
- Expand the construction, assignment, leader-follow and preliminary courier GameTests and add single-player and dedicated-server tests, including time skips, starvation, save migration, multiple workers, missing resources, construction sites and unreachable paths. Test resource reload and villager POI/trades, too.

Some path requests occurred while the villager was airborne, when ground navigation cannot calculate a route; an immediate-on-landing retry and exposing the work path to vanilla door AI did not by themselves eliminate the integration failure. The pathfinder now avoids water while searching for a shore route rather than rejecting a wet shortcut, and a focused detour test plus two CI runs passed; more repeat runs and in-game validation are needed before calling the intermittent failure resolved. Courier and guard tests were intermittently affected by storage and hostiles in nearby test structures. Fixtures were isolated; guards additionally required their combat navigation to be protected from vanilla panic. These tests need repeated runs. Passing isolated GameTests is not a substitute for single-player and dedicated-server integration testing. No overhaul release has been published.

## Starter and forester follow-up

The `/colony spawn` command now looks for safe loaded plots for both a town hall and a farm before creating a colony; it reports failure rather than silently creating one without the hall. The hall can be away from the exact command position. `/colony repair <id>` attempts a missing hall only at a safe original center and reports if it cannot; it does not remove existing buildings. New colonies start with one stone pickaxe and stone axe in virtual stock. The miner can withdraw a pickaxe from colony stock, using its durability; this is not yet a physical trip to collect it. A smith can make replacements from real materials. Existing colonies do not automatically receive new tools.

The forester's conservative short, straight-trunk recognition now also recognizes several other overworld log/leaf species and uses the matching stocked sapling where a single sapling can grow. This does **not** yet meet the requirement to clear all tree types: tall, branched, multi-trunk and many natural tree forms remain unsupported; dark oak cannot be replanted as a single sapling. Species does not prove that a log was naturally generated: player property near worksites remains at risk. Targeted starter, spruce and birch GameTests passed in run 43386903053, but the full run failed on the previously intermittent harbor-to-shore fishing test. No new jar was uploaded by that failed run.
