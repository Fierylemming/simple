# Overhaul in progress — not a release

The user requested a single completed release. This branch is a work in progress, not a distribution-ready mod or jar recommendation. Do not treat compilation as proof of in-game correctness. Back up worlds and use a disposable test world if testing the branch.

## Decisions

- `/time` catches up daily food and other daily effects, but does not fast-forward walking, gathering or building. Large skips can starve citizens.
- A new settlement begins with a town hall, food, a farm and four workstation blocks; there are no initial beds. Villagers should shelter in the hall or houses at night.
- Three level-one houses per culture; expansion should respond to needs and affordable materials rather than a fixed day timer.
- User requested a single completed release, including accurate physical work for every profession and social behaviours.

## Work started

Expansion now checks on normal colony ticks, reports blockers and does not stall on the previously unsupported next-building type. It queues an affordable house if shelter capacity is low. Construction can craft a small set of oak components and has preliminary movement, with an occupied-placement check. Legacy unsupported blueprints no longer block house expansion. Cached stock is refreshed against loaded inventories before consumption. Current v7 houses retain the v6 three-footprint plans (including an L shape) and distinct culture-dependent walls and roof silhouettes, using oak and cobblestone; older versions remain available on reload. These designs and their in-game accessibility are not yet verified. New settlements attempt to use the starter structure as a bedless town hall; a preliminary nighttime shelter navigation behaviour and crop harvesting/replanting using actual drops were added. Farmers also craft bread from harvested wheat; uncooked wheat is no longer counted as a meal. Woodcutters now approach and cut one block at a time for short recognizable trees, recheck safety between cuts, and use stock saplings rather than creating them. Workstation-based crafting jobs now approach the claimed POI; a basic stock-backed baker has been added. The butcher no longer fabricates meat: it attacks excess unnamed adult cows or pigs near a completed butcher hut, leaves a pair, and collects only actual unmodified livestock drops from its own attack when they spawn within reach. It feeds eligible breeding pairs with real stock and relies on vanilla animal AI to produce offspring, subject to a small herd cap. These are not yet verified in a running game.

## Required before release

- Verify/fix the startup town hall, compact farm, pathfinding, door placement, roofs, resource accounting and 15 house plans in-game, including reload and blocked/unloaded sites. The initial town hall and house designs are provisional and not historically complete.
- Test the builder, forester, mine, smithy and butcher worksite shells around claimed POIs, including site separation, obstructed/legacy sites, materials, pathfinding and reloads. The v7 smithy includes a builder-crafted adjacent furnace; v6 and older unsupported smithy/butcher placeholders remain unchanged on load. Improve site planning and growth for additional building types; these are the only non-house construction plans so far.
- Finish and test physical tree harvesting. A preliminary short-tree implementation now saves partial-tree progress, revalidates safety after reload, and uses real axes with durability; hand cutting remains a compatibility fallback for old saves. Focused GameTests cover resuming after an entity save, axe wear and refusing to continue after new construction; broader testing is still needed. A preliminary miner now walks a descending quarry and uses a real pickaxe; its focused GameTest has passed but quarry safety, reload, site exhaustion and natural-world navigation still need coverage. Avoid damage to buildings or player property; natural-block recognition remains heuristic.
- Finish and test every profession's real supply-chain behaviour, including butcher, leatherworker, blacksmith, baker, fisherman, courier, soldier/guard and any required shepherd/wool system. The blacksmith now crafts capped spare stone or iron tools from real inputs at a claimed workstation, including sticks from oak stock; it now uses a real adjacent furnace in a designated blacksmith building, consumes actual fuel and ore, and persists batch ownership on the furnace. Automated smelting is limited to raw iron and requires broader integration testing. The leatherworker crafts a single spare chestplate at its claimed workstation using eight leather, but does not deliver or equip armor. A preliminary courier now carries a persisted stack between storage containers and a designated hub; other stock access is still remote and may include player containers. Current nonstarter professions are not distribution-ready.
- Expand preliminary idle gossip conversations into proper social interactions and implement robust day/night navigation. Shelter selection now checks a solid floor, clear headroom, nearby roof and reachable interior, with fallback to other completed houses or halls. It does not verify full enclosure, prevent hostile entry, reserve space or guarantee villagers remain inside all night. The GameTests exercise a bedless hall and two villagers avoiding a roofless marker and sealed house for a constructed house; full-night and in-game tests remain necessary. Address beds and actual food production/cooking beyond wheat counts.
- Expand the construction GameTests (and preliminary courier GameTest) and add single-player and dedicated-server tests, including time skips, starvation, save migration, multiple workers, missing resources, construction sites and unreachable paths. Test resource reload and villager POI/trades, too.

## Latest test status

CI on the work-in-progress branch has compiled the mod and exercised house variants, claimed-POI expansion, worksite shells, miner, courier, guard, farmer, woodcutter, blacksmith, furnace batches, butcher, cattle and pig breeding, and bedless shelter, including a two-villager reachable-house fallback. The miner test passes after removing a GameTest-only barrier ceiling and verifies that the worker advances down the quarry. Woodcutter save/reload, unsafe-site and tool-durability tests and a tool-reassignment test also pass in CI. Courier and guard tests were intermittently affected by storage and hostiles in nearby test structures. Fixtures were isolated; guards additionally required their combat navigation to be protected from vanilla panic. These tests need repeated runs. Passing isolated GameTests is not a substitute for single-player and dedicated-server integration testing. No overhaul release has been published.
