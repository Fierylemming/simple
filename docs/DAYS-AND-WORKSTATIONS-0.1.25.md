# Day skips and workstations (0.1.25)

Back up the world before testing. Colony days are processed sequentially, at most 64 per colony update (normally every 100 game ticks), while the colony center is loaded. A 50-day jump is processed on the next update; very large jumps take multiple updates. Food is consumed for each day, so an unfed colony can starve during a jump. Health days for unloaded citizens are saved and applied when they load. Work, construction, crop growth and vanilla entity aging are **not** fast-forwarded; they still need actual game ticks. Reversing time resets the day cursor rather than undoing simulation.

Fresh starter colonies get a composter at the farm, plus an anvil (builder), furnace (miner), and unlit campfire (woodcutter), provided a safe plot can be found. Existing colonies can use `/colony repair <id>` to add missing starter stations, or place them manually inside the colony. One villager claims each station; job assignment remains when the station is missing, and work resumes after a free matching station is found. Other job stations: butcher/baker smoker, leatherworker cauldron, blacksmith smithing table, soldier/guard grindstone, fisherman/courier barrel. Some of those jobs are not implemented yet. Don't relight the woodcutter campfire.

Trading uses vanilla-style offers for each colony profession, borrowed from corresponding vanilla trade lists. Reassigning a job closes any current trade and resets offers, XP, and level. Existing pre-0.1.25 villagers are migrated on load; their old vanilla offers are replaced. Current profession overlay textures are transparent placeholders; the name and profession are synchronized, but distinctive outfits are not yet implemented.

Suggested checks: load a colony and note `/colony status`; run `/time add 50d`, wait 5 seconds, check food, health, and expansion countdown. Break the builder anvil and confirm `WAITING_FOR_WORKSTATION`; replace it and confirm work resumes. Assign two builders with only one anvil and check only one works. Trade, reassign, and check the new trades and reset level. Reload and repeat. Large skips do not simulate construction between days, so only one unfinished house can be queued.
