# Woodcutters and villager outfits (0.1.27)

Back up your world before trying this update. Colony villagers now render existing vanilla profession overlays from Minecraft resources rather than the transparent placeholder images. Farmer uses the vanilla farmer outfit and hat; other custom jobs use a comparable vanilla outfit (for example, woodcutter uses fletcher and builder uses mason). Changing jobs changes the overlay; unemployed villagers and babies do not show a job outfit. Resource packs that replace vanilla outfits continue to apply. Distinct custom outfits are not yet available.

Woodcutters no longer treat a random log near leaves as a tree. They look for an upright, rooted oak trunk with an attached natural canopy and reject nearby construction, horizontal/adjacent logs, player-placed leaves, fluids, unloaded space, known colony buildings, and generated structure bounding boxes. The whole validated trunk is harvested together and an oak sapling is planted at its base when possible. They can also plant saplings in clear, unprotected ground. The checks are deliberately conservative: oak trees near buildings, close to other trees, or with branches may be skipped. Other tree species are not harvested yet. The detection is heuristic; an unregistered player build deliberately resembling an isolated natural tree cannot be distinguished perfectly, so keep valuable builds and woodcutters separated or register buildings with a marker.

The old fallback that made idle farmers and woodcutters break nearby stone has been disabled because it could damage buildings; initial colony stock still includes cobblestone. This does not repair damage already done. Replace damaged blocks manually from a backup or in-game.

Manual checks: verify a farmer gets the straw hat and outfit, change their job and confirm the outfit changes, then reload. Put a mature oak away from buildings and one next to a farm or a registered house; the isolated tree should be cut and replanted while the building and nearby tree remain intact. Also test a wooden house under leaves, a farm fence and a house within a generated structure, with multiple woodcutters and after reloading. No in-game test has yet been performed for these changes.
